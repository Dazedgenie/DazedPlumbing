description=[h1]Dazed Plumbing[/h1]
description=[b]Tanks, pipes, pumps and running water for Build 42.[/b]
description=Store water, propane and petrol, pipe them where you need them, and bring a whole house's taps back to life after the water shuts off.

description=[h2]Tanks[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]18 tanks[/b]: small, large and extra-large, for water, propane or petrol, in a [b]Salvaged[/b] tier (found or rigged from scrap) and a welded [b]Crafted[/b] tier.
description=[*]Pour in, take out into bottles and cans, and patch leaks. Damaged tanks leak, even while you are away.
description=[*]Propane and petrol tanks explode and burn if fire gets too close.
description=[*]Open-topped tanks catch rain. A gauge window shows level, quality and what feeds and draws from each tank.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Pipe networks[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Right-click a machine, choose its line, and [b]Pipe to[/b] a tank. Runs that meet join automatically, so one line can feed many machines and tanks.
description=[*][b]Colour-coded and never mixed[/b]: water blue, propane white, petrol red.
description=[*][b]Shut-off valves[/b] to close any line. Pipes are built from pipe sections and need Welding.
description=[*]Indoors they run overhead, outdoors on the ground. Outdoor pipes wear under zombies and vehicles and need mending.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Water for your base[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Water main[/b] – stand it outside a house, pick the building, and every sink, bath, shower, toilet and washer inside runs from your tank once the town water is off.
description=[*][b]Taps[/b] – pipe a single fixture straight to a tank.
description=[*][b]Hand and electric water pumps[/b] draw ground water. A [b]drilled well[/b] pumps about 15 L of clean water a minute on Dazed Power.
description=[*][b]Purifier[/b] – cleans dirty water on its way into a tank.
description=[*][b]Rain collection[/b] – barrels beside a building catch its roof, [b]downspouts[/b] feed a tank from the whole roof, and vanilla rain barrels can be piped in.
description=[*][b]Garden sprinklers[/b] water crops within 3 squares, with a schedule (dawn, evening, night) and skip-when-raining.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Fuel and gas[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Fuel pump[/b] – refuel a parked car or fill petrol cans from a piped petrol tank. Hand-cranked, or electric with Dazed Power.
description=[*][b]Biogas digester[/b] – feed it rotten food and manure and it slowly makes propane. Slower in the cold, stops below freezing.
description=[*][b]Smoker[/b] – a propane-fired box that smokes raw meat and fish so it keeps six times longer. Works with Dazed Butchery cuts.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Works with[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Dazed Power[/b] – propane and petrol generators burn straight from a tank on their line, steam engines are kept topped up with water, and electric pumps, the purifier, the drilled well and the electric fuel pump run off your controller.
description=[*][b]Dazed Dank[/b] – pipe a hydro reservoir to a water tank to fill and top it up.
description=[*][b]Dazed Climate[/b] (coming soon) – pipes and tanks freeze, and a tank that freezes too full can crack.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
