description=Tanks, pipes, pumps and running water for [b]Build 42[/b]. Store water, propane and petrol, pipe them where you need them, and bring a whole house's taps back to life after the water shuts off.
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][td][b]Game version[/b][/td][td]Build 42 only (42.0+)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Requires[/b][/td][td][b]Dazed Core[/b] 1.4.0 or newer (subscribe to it too)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Multiplayer[/b][/td][td]Built for it, server is the authority. Not yet tested on a dedicated server, reports welcome[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Add mid-save[/b][/td][td]Yes[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Works with[/b][/td][td]Dazed Power, Dazed Climate, Dazed Dank, Dazed Butchery, Dazed Cooking[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Mod ID[/b][/td][td]DazedPlumbing[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/h-tanks.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/tanks.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]18 tanks[/b]: small, large and extra-large, for water, propane or petrol, in a [b]Salvaged[/b] tier (found or rigged from scrap) and a welded [b]Crafted[/b] tier.
description=[*]Pour in, take out into bottles and cans, patch leaks and rename tanks. Damaged tanks leak, even while you are away.
description=[*]Propane and petrol tanks explode and burn if fire gets too close.
description=[*]Open-topped tanks catch rain. A gauge window shows level, quality and what feeds and draws from each tank.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/h-pipes.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/pipes.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Right-click a machine, choose its line, and [b]Pipe to[/b] a tank. Runs that meet join with T-branches, so one line can feed many machines and tanks.
description=[*][b]Colour-coded and never mixed[/b]: water blue, propane white, petrol red.
description=[*][b]Shut-off valves[/b] close any line. Pipes are welded from pipe sections, and can be cut and mended.
description=[*]Indoors they run overhead, outdoors on the ground. Outdoor pipes wear under zombies and vehicles and need mending.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/h-water.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/water.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Water main[/b]: stand it outside a house, pick the building in the [b]Building Picker[/b], and every sink, bath, shower, toilet and washer inside runs from your tank once the town water is off.
description=[*][b]Water panel[/b]: right-click a connected main to open a board with supply and demand dials, the house's line rate, every tank and source on the line, per-fixture valves and fill order, and a main shut-off with an optional drain. A wall panel hung inside opens it from indoors.
description=[*][b]Taps[/b] pipe a single fixture straight to a tank. Tainted water stays tainted.
description=[*][b]Hand and electric water pumps[/b] on bare ground draw ground water into a tank.
description=[*][b]Drilled well[/b]: about 15 L of clean water a minute, wired to Dazed Power.
description=[*][b]Purifier[/b] cleans dirty water on its way into a tank, fast with a filter cartridge fitted.
description=[*][b]Rain collection[/b]: open tanks catch rain, barrels beside a building catch its roof runoff, [b]downspouts[/b] feed a tank from the whole roof, and vanilla rain barrels can be piped in.
description=[*][b]Garden sprinklers[/b] water thirsty crops within 3 squares on a schedule: Always, Dawn, Evening or Night, with Skip when raining.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/h-fuel.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/fuel.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Fuel pumps[/b]: refuel a parked car or fill petrol cans from a piped petrol tank. Hand-cranked, or electric with Dazed Power.
description=[*][b]Biogas digester[/b]: feed it rotten food and manure and it slowly makes propane. Half speed below 10 °C, stops below freezing.
description=[*][b]Smoker[/b]: a propane-fired drum that smokes raw meat and fish in 8 hours, so it keeps six times longer.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/h-works.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Dazed Power[/b]: propane and petrol generators burn straight from a tank on their line, steam engines are kept topped up with water, and the pumps, purifier, drilled well and electric fuel pump run off your controller.
description=[*][b]Dazed Climate[/b]: pipes and tanks freeze in the cold, and pipe insulation slows it. A tank frozen over 80% full can crack and leak until you weld it.
description=[*][b]Dazed Dank[/b]: pipe a hydro reservoir to a water tank to fill and top it up.
description=[*][b]Dazed Butchery[/b] and [b]Dazed Cooking[/b]: the smoker smokes Butchery cuts and dries jerky strips.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Plumbing/images/h-sandbox.png?v=2[/img]
description=Water main reach and flow rate, and whether pipe work needs a pipe wrench. Or pick one [b]preset[/b] (Easy, Standard, Realistic, Hardcore) on the Dazed Core page to set every Dazed mod at once.
description=
