# Dazed Plumbing -- roadmap

## Ideas (added 2026-10-10, from the community scan; not designed or coded)

- No-skill rain catcher: a tarp-and-bucket catcher anyone can build without Carpentry, smaller than a barrel, for
  early water.
- Lake and river intake: a pump line that draws from open water into a tank or to the sprinklers. Check first how much
  the ground pumps already cover (they hold 1600 L near open water).
- Stored water goes bad: open tanks and standing pipes slowly turn tainted unless covered or fed through the purifier;
  taps in a house not on the water main drain and go stale after the shutoff.
